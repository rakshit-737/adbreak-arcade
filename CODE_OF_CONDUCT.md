# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

AdBreak Arcade is a small, welcoming project. We expect everyone who
participates in the project to:

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Assume good intent while staying open to feedback.
- Focus criticism on code, documentation, or ideas rather than people.
- Give credit to others' work and respect different backgrounds and
  experience levels.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in project spaces:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, doxxing, or publishing private information.
- Spam, impersonation, or attempts to manipulate project discussions.
- Sharing credentials, private vulnerability details, or other sensitive data.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional technical
  community.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests,
discussions, project website, and other spaces where someone is representing
AdBreak Arcade.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainer privately through the
maintainer's GitHub profile: <https://github.com/rakshit-737>.

Please do not open a public issue for sensitive conduct reports. Include the
relevant context, links, dates, and any supporting material. Reports will be
reviewed as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may remove comments or contributions, close discussions,
restrict participation, or take other action they consider necessary to keep
the project safe and constructive. Retaliation against someone who reports a
concern in good faith is not acceptable.

This policy is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
